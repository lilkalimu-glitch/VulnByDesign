About VulnByDesign

What is VulnByDesign?

VulnByDesign is an intentionally vulnerable web application built for security enthusiasts, students, and professionals to practice identifying and exploiting common web vulnerabilities in a safe, legal environment.

Each lab focuses on a specific vulnerability class from the OWASP Top 10 and includes:

  • A realistic vulnerable feature to exploit
  • Hints to guide your testing
  • Explanations of the root cause and remediation
